# Partner with Applied AI Society

There is one simple door into AAS partnership work: **start a conversation in the Applied AI Society Discord.**

Whether you are:

- An operator who wants to [**get your company Jarvised**](/docs/get-your-company-jarvised)
- A business owner exploring how AI could reshape your operations
- A sponsor interested in backing a local chapter
- A school, foundation, or community wanting to bring AAS to your people
- Someone with a partnership idea that does not fit an existing template

The first step is the same. Reach out. AAS leadership will help you directly if the fit is right, or connect you with someone in the AAS practitioner network whose situation and timeline matches yours.
