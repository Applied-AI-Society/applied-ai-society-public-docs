# Operational Reality

*Everything that is actually true about your world right now. The substrate your AI needs to be a real co-strategist.*

---

## What It Is

Your operational reality is the full picture of what is happening in your life and work at this moment.

It includes:

- **Your mission.** What you are actually trying to do, at multiple time horizons.
- **Your status quo.** Where you are today. What is working. What is not.
- **The people around you.** Your team, your clients, your advisors, your network, your adversaries. Their contexts, their needs, their histories with you.
- **Your challenges and blockers.** What is actually stuck. What is waiting on what. What keeps you up at night.
- **Your opportunities.** The leads, invitations, and open doors you have not yet walked through.
- **Your competitors.** Who is in your space, what they are doing, where they are ahead or behind.
- **Your constraints.** Time, capital, energy, team capacity, values, season of life.

This is the substrate. Strategy that is not informed by your operational reality is strategy in a vacuum. And strategy in a vacuum is indistinguishable from noise.

## Why It Matters

**AI can only co-strategize with you to the extent that it understands what is actually going on.**

A friend who has not talked to you in a year cannot advise you on today's decision. Neither can an AI that only knows you through a fresh chat window. The difference between a useful co-strategist and a generic answer-machine is how much operational context the AI can read.

This is why [context engineering](/docs/concepts/context-engineering) is the core applied AI skill, and why [Personal Agentic OS](/docs/concepts/personal-agentic-os) exists as a concept. You are building the substrate. The AI reads the substrate. The strategy gets better.

## Operational Reality Is Not Static

The hard part: your operational reality changes every day. New conversations, new decisions, new information, shifts in the people around you, markets that moved overnight.

So operational reality is not a thing you write down once. It is a living layer you maintain. This is the work of [legacy](/docs/legacy). It is the reason [capture, process, compound](/docs/concepts/capture-process-compound) is a daily practice, not a one-time setup.

When your documented operational reality falls out of sync with what is actually happening, you develop [agentic OS debt](/docs/concepts/agentic-os-debt). Your AI co-strategist is advising based on yesterday's world, and you are living in today's.

## What Good Operational Reality Looks Like In Practice

- **Meeting notes ingested automatically** into your [context lake](/docs/concepts/context-lake). (Granola to your agent, for example.)
- **Relationship files** for every meaningful person in your orbit, updated after significant interactions.
- **Strategic docs** for every active project, refreshed when the strategy changes.
- **Decision logs** for the non-trivial choices you make, with reasoning captured.
- **A current user profile** that says who you are, what you value, what you are trying to do, and what season you are in.

You do not need all of this to start. You need enough to make your AI meaningfully more useful than a blank chat window. Begin with a user profile and a few relationship dossiers. Add from there.

## Why Document It

**The truth that matters is the truth that exists in files an AI can read.**

If it is only in your head, your AI cannot use it. If your AI cannot use it, you are leaving most of the leverage on the table. This is the point of [externalizing your brain](/docs/concepts/externalize-your-brain): make your operational reality legible to the tools that can multiply it.

Documented operational reality is also what lets you [see your own thinking](/docs/concepts/see-your-own-thinking). The act of writing it down often clarifies what you did not realize you believed. The AI reading it back to you is the second pass.
