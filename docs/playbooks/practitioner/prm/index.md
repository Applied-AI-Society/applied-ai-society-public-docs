# PRM Tutorials

*Hands-on guides for building, feeding, and operating your [Personal Relationship Management](/docs/concepts/prm) layer inside your [Personal Agentic OS](/docs/concepts/personal-agentic-os).*

---

The PRM concept page covers the why and the shape of the system: dossiers, transcripts, and artifacts cross-linked over years. These tutorials cover the how: the specific moves that get conversations out of your meeting tools and into your workspace, in a form your Jarvis can reason over.

## Available Tutorials

| Tutorial | What It Covers |
|----------|---------------|
| [Auto-Syncing Granola Notes](/docs/playbooks/practitioner/prm/auto-syncing-granola-notes) | Skill that pulls recent Granola meetings into `meeting-transcripts/` on demand. Plus the manual fallback (TurboScribe) for voice memos and wearable recorders like Plaud. |

## What's Next

These tutorials grow as the [Minimum Viable Jarvis template](https://github.com/Applied-AI-Society/minimum-viable-jarvis) gains new ingestion skills. Anything that puts conversation context into the workspace lands here.
