# Get Your Company Jarvised

*Train up every employee on your team with the Jarvis workshop. Every person leaves with their own Personal Agentic OS. From there, the team compounds.*

---

## What This Page Is

This is the company-level version of [Get Jarvised](/docs/get-jarvised). The individual page is for one person deciding to level up. This one is for the operator, founder, or exec who wants their whole team operating at the same level.

The simplest form of this: we run the [Supersuit Up workshop](/docs/workshops/supersuit-up) for your team. Every person walks out with a working Jarvis on their laptop, configured to their role. From there, the team compounds.

---

## Why Training Up the Team Is the Move

One person with a Jarvis is a level up. A whole team with Jarvises is a step function.

- **Shared vocabulary.** Everyone understands what a skill file is, what context lake means, what primed prompts look like. Meetings get shorter.
- **Shared skill library.** One person figures out the perfect newsletter-drafting skill; everyone else has it next week. That is the [Raise the Floor](/docs/concepts/raise-the-floor) mechanic at team scale.
- **Compounding institutional knowledge.** When every employee is writing to persistent files the agents can read, the company's operational reality builds itself into a [Sovereign Agentic Business OS](/docs/concepts/sovereign-agentic-business-os) over time. You do not need a big engineering budget to get there. You need everyone Jarvised and a shared posture toward documentation.
- **Defense against AI-native competitors.** [Ramp rolled this out to 700 employees](/docs/case-studies/ramp-glass) and shipped the result as an actual product (Glass). The gap between teams who have done this and teams who have not is already visible and widens every quarter.

---

## Why Now

If you lead a team, the most important decision you will make in the next 12 months is whether your whole team operates with real AI leverage. Not one power user. Everyone.

The fastest way to make that shift is the workshop. Every employee builds their own Jarvis from scratch, on their own laptop, with their own context. The same [Supersuit Up workshop](/docs/workshops/supersuit-up) we run publicly, delivered inside your company so every person on your team hits day one of their compounding system at the same time.

No custom deliverables. No org redesign. Just the training, clean, so your team has the foundation. Everything that compounds from there compounds because your team owns the foundation, not because they hired a consultant to think for them.
