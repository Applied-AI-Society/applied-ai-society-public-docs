# Who We Serve

*If you are humble and hungry, using AI as a glorified Google is not getting what you deserve from this revolution. This page is here to say: we see you, and this is for you. The bar is real and we hold it honestly. See [sustained commercial success](/docs/concepts/sustained-commercial-success) for why that bar exists and what it requires of you.*

---

## This Is For You

If you want to 10x so you can stay relevant at the company that employs you, **this is for you**.

If you want to build the best possible company in the world, **this is for you**.

If you want to make more money in a soulful way, **this is for you**.

If you want to create the best art of your life, **this is for you**.

If you want to be a more human human in the AI age, **this is for you**.

Applied AI Society exists to walk you from "I have heard of AI and use ChatGPT or Claude Cowork" to "I am operating with AI in a way that is genuinly changing how I live for the better."

We call that getting [activated](/docs/concepts/activation). [Getting Jarvised](/docs/get-jarvised) is the mechanism.

---

## The Four Communities

We are built around four groups. Three of them are the people we primarily serve. The fourth is the community that keeps the offering for the first three honest, battle-tested, and at the frontier. You almost certainly see yourself in one of these.

### 1. Non-Developer Business Leaders of the Future

Business owners, operators, executives, and founders in industries where the primary value creation is not shipping code. Real estate, healthcare, law, coaching, services, retail, construction, hospitality, media.

For you, a [Personal Agentic OS](/docs/concepts/personal-agentic-os) is a 10x on the judgment, communication, and strategy you already get paid for. You need infrastructure that carries everything below your highest-value judgment, so more of your day lives inside the work only you can do. No engineering skills required.

Your future hire is one group down on this page. More on that in the [Humanity-First Flywheel](/docs/about/humanity-first-flywheel).

### 2. Artists

Musicians, filmmakers, writers, painters, poets, directors, designers. The heart of humanity's creative culture.

AI supercomputers are the biggest marginal unlock you have seen in your lifetime. An artist who barely uses a computer today, once [Jarvised](/docs/get-jarvised), can produce a different order of magnitude of creative output tomorrow. Worldbuilding, production, distribution, business operations, everything downstream of the art, amplified so the art itself can get more of your time and presence.

We take artists seriously because culture is downstream of who shapes it. A future where only developers have agentic infrastructure is a culturally poorer future for everyone, and we are working to prevent it.

### 3. Non-Developer Students

Undergraduates, graduate students, and early-career people who plan to make their living in roles other than full-time software engineering. Business, policy, medicine, design, the arts, the trades, the social sector.

The economy is shifting under your feet. Entry-level knowledge work is being absorbed into agentic workflows, and the companies that employ the business leaders above need young talent who can work this way. That is you. You are the bridge. Your emerging applied AI practice is worth more than most of your peers realize, and our job is to help you turn it into a career that compounds. See [applied AI economy](/docs/playbooks/practitioner/applied-ai-economy) and [five levels of value](/docs/playbooks/student/five-levels-of-value) for the concrete paths.

### 4. Top-Tier Applied AI Engineering Talent

Builders. Engineers, researchers, skilled open-source contributors, framework authors. The people who can tell whether a curriculum is cutting edge, outdated, or [slop](/docs/concepts/slopacalypse).

Your role here is upstream: keep the workshops, docs, and skills honest and at the frontier. Sanity-check what gets published. Contribute skills and harness patterns. Surface edge cases. Show the non-technical community what the real frontier looks like this month, not last year. In return, you get a trusted ecosystem of non-technical leaders, artists, and students who hire you at commercial rates once they know what good applied AI work looks like.

If that sounds like a trade you want to be part of, read the [Humanity-First Flywheel](/docs/about/humanity-first-flywheel) next. It is how the four communities compound value for each other.

## How to Plug In

- **For anyone:** Read the [canonical invitation](/docs/get-jarvised) and pick the path with the lowest activation energy for you this week.
- **For business leaders:** [Get your company Jarvised](/docs/get-your-company-jarvised).
- **For students:** Start with [five levels of value](/docs/playbooks/student/five-levels-of-value), then see the [applied AI economy map](/docs/playbooks/practitioner/applied-ai-economy).
- **For artists:** Come to a [Supersuit Up workshop](/docs/workshops/supersuit-up). Personal Agentic OS first. The creative tools come alive once the operating layer is in place.
- **For builders:** Contribute upstream. The [docs are source-controlled](https://github.com/Applied-AI-Society). Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/K7uWJBMFaN), open PRs, and we will route you to the conversations that matter.
- **For everyone:** Subscribe to the [Applied AI Society newsletter](https://appliedaisociety.substack.com/). Biweekly field notes from the frontier.
